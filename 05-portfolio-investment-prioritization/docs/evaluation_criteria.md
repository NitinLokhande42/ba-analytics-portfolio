# Evaluation Criteria (agreed by R&D, Marketing, Manufacturing, Finance)

| Criterion | Weight | Measure | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expected NPV per ₹ invested | 30% | Mean simulated NPV ÷ capex+launch cost | Finance |
| Downside risk | 20% | P(NPV < 0) | Finance |
| Payback | 10% | Years to cumulative cash break-even (median) | Finance |
| Therapy strategic fit | 15% | 1–5: alignment with focus therapies | Marketing |
| Capability fit | 15% | 1–5: existing plant, technology, field force | Manufacturing / R&D |
| Competitive intensity | 10% | 1–5 (5 = low competition) | Marketing |

**Hard rules**
- P(NPV < 0) > 40% → not funded in current form; return with mitigation.
- Regulatory pathway must be confirmed by Regulatory Affairs before Gate 2.
- All assumptions must come from the shared register (no project-specific discount rates).
