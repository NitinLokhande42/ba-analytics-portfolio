# Manager Training & Rollout Plan

| Session | Audience | Content | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Why segmentation | Zonal & regional managers | Business case, pilot results, segment definitions | 1.5 h |
| 2. Using the call plan in CRM | Area managers | Segment field, call targets, override process | 2 h |
| 3. Coaching with data | Area managers | Adherence report in Power BI, joint-field-work checklist | 2 h |
| 4. Rep cascade | Reps (led by managers) | What changes in their week; FAQs | 1 h |

**Support:** FAQ document, weekly office hours for 6 weeks, adherence tracked per manager.
**Success criteria:** adherence ≥ 80% by month 3 of rollout; fewer than 5% override requests.
