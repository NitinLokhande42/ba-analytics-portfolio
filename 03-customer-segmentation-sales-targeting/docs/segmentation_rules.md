# Segmentation Rules (agreed with Sales Leadership)

## Inputs
| Variable | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Potential | Monthly prescriptions written by the doctor in Cipla's priority therapy areas (all brands) | Prescription audit |
| Cipla share | Cipla prescriptions ÷ total prescriptions in those therapy areas | Prescription audit |
| Specialty | Consulting physician, cardiologist, pulmonologist, diabetologist, GP | Doctor master |

## Tiers
- **Potential tier:** High = top 20% of doctors in the territory; Medium = next 30%; Low = bottom 50%.
- **Share tier:** High ≥ 30%; Medium 10–30%; Low < 10%.

## Segment grid
| Potential \ Share | Low share | Medium share | High share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High** | GROW | GROW | DEFEND |
| **Medium** | DEVELOP | MAINTAIN | DEFEND |
| **Low** | DEPRIORITISE | DEPRIORITISE | MAINTAIN |

**Priority segments** for the program: GROW and DEFEND.

## Governance
- Segments refreshed quarterly from the latest audit data.
- A rep can request an override (max 5% of list) with manager approval, logged in CRM.
- Doctors with no audit data default to MAINTAIN until data is available.
