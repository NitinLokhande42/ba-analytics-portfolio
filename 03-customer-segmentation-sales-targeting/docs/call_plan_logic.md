# Call-Plan Logic

| Segment | Calls / month | Priority products | Activities |
|---|---|---|---|
| GROW | 4 | Lead brands in under-penetrated therapy | Detailing, samples, CME invites |
| DEFEND | 3 | Current lead brands | Reminder detailing, patient support programs |
| DEVELOP | 2 | Lead brand | Detailing |
| MAINTAIN | 1 | Portfolio reminder | Short call / digital touch |
| DEPRIORITISE | 0 (digital only) | — | Email / WhatsApp content |

## Capacity rules
1. Rep capacity = 10 calls/day × 20 working days = **200 calls/month**.
2. Fill in order GROW → DEFEND → DEVELOP → MAINTAIN, highest potential first within each segment.
3. If capacity runs out, reduce MAINTAIN to 0.5 (alternate months) before touching GROW/DEFEND.
4. If capacity is left over, add DEVELOP doctors from neighbouring territories (manager decision).

## Adherence KPI
Call-plan adherence = planned calls completed on the right doctor ÷ planned calls. Target ≥ 80%.
