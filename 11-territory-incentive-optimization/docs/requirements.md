# Requirements — Territory & Incentive Optimization

| ID | Requirement | Source | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-1 | Workload per territory within ±15% of the national average | Sales leadership | Must |
| R-2 | Each rep keeps their top-10 accounts by revenue (continuity) | Sales leadership | Must |
| R-3 | No more than 20% of a territory's accounts change hands | Sales ops | Must |
| R-4 | Accounts only move to an adjacent territory (travel) | Sales ops | Must |
| R-5 | Targets = 60% potential share + 40% historical share of national target | Finance | Must |
| R-6 | Payout calculated monthly, paid quarterly; statement per rep | HR / Finance | Must |
| R-7 | Every payout rule has a written definition and a test case | Sales ops | Must |
| R-8 | Dispute log with reason codes and resolution time | HR | Should |
| R-9 | Power BI: territory workload map, attainment and payout by rep (RLS: rep sees own) | Sales ops | Should |
