# Monthly Demand Review — New Format

**Duration:** 90 minutes (was 3 hours) · **Chair:** Head of S&OP · **Pre-read:** dashboard pages 1, 2, 5 shared D+6

| Time | Agenda item |
|---|---|
| 10 min | Last month accuracy and bias headline |
| 20 min | Exceptions only: A-class SKUs with accuracy < 70% or bias > ±10% |
| 30 min | Brand overrides above ±15% of statistical — rationale and evidence |
| 20 min | Stockout-risk list and actions |
| 10 min | Consensus sign-off and lock |

**Adoption measures:** attendance, % overrides with reason codes (target 100%), FVA positive for ≥ 60% of overrides,
dashboard used as the only source in the meeting (no side spreadsheets after month 3).
