# Stakeholder Interview Synthesis (15 interviews)

| # | Role | Key needs | Pain points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chief Commercial Officer | One view of revenue vs. target, by region, daily | Conflicting numbers in leadership meetings |
| 2–5 | Regional heads (4) | Territory performance, top/bottom accounts, margin | Wait 3 days after month end; numbers change after review |
| 6–7 | Finance controllers (2) | Margin bridge, price/volume/mix | Manual reconciliation to ledger every month |
| 8–9 | Sales ops (2) | Target tracking, incentive inputs | Maintain 20+ spreadsheets |
| 10–12 | Key account managers (3) | Account history, pipeline, open orders | Data in 4 systems |
| 13 | Head of FP&A | Forecast vs. actual | No pipeline linkage |
| 14 | IT data lead | Fewer bespoke extracts | 40+ scheduled jobs for reports |
| 15 | Marketing lead | Product performance by segment | No access to sales data |

## Themes
1. **Single source of truth** — 11 of 15 named conflicting definitions as the top issue.
2. **Timeliness** — 9 of 15 want daily data; month-end in < 1 day.
3. **Drill-down** — from total to account and invoice in three clicks.
4. **Security** — regions must not see each other's accounts; margin restricted to finance and leadership.
5. **Less effort** — analysts spend ~40% of time producing reports.
