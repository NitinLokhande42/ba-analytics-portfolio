# Retention Workflow

| Tier | Score band | Action | Owner | SLA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High | Top 10% (p ≥ threshold_high) | Proactive call by senior agent; root-cause fix; offer from playbook | Retention desk | 3 business days |
| Medium | Next 15% | Personalised email + in-app guidance; call if no response | Customer success | 7 days |
| Low | Rest | Standard lifecycle communications | Marketing automation | — |

## Offer playbook (by top reason code)
| Reason | Action |
|---|---|
| Usage decline | Training session, feature adoption plan |
| Support pain (many tickets / low CSAT) | Escalation manager, fix open tickets first |
| Price sensitivity | Plan right-sizing; discount only with manager approval (max 15%) |
| Low adoption of key module | Onboarding refresh |

## Logging
Every contact logged in CRM with outcome code (saved, will think, cancelled anyway, unreachable). Outcome feeds model retraining.
