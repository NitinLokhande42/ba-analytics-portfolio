# Phased Rollout

| Phase | Scope | Duration | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Pilot | One department (~60 users) | 4 weeks | Cycle time ≤ 2 days; no critical defects; user satisfaction ≥ 4/5 |
| 1. Finance & procurement | Core approvers + 3 departments | 4 weeks | Matrix validated for all cost centres |
| 2. Company-wide | All departments; email route closed | 6 weeks | > 95% requests through app; paper forms withdrawn |
| 3. Hypercare | Support desk, weekly fixes | 4 weeks | Ticket volume < 5/week |

**Change management:** 20-minute videos, quick-reference card, approver champions per floor, weekly adoption dashboard.
